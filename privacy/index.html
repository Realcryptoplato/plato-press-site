Privacy

A plain-language overview of how the Plato Press website handles information.

The studio website

Plato Press does not sell personal information or use it for behavioral advertising. Cloudflare hosts the website and may process technical information such as an IP address, browser type, and requested pages for security, delivery, and reliability.

Email signup

The pages on this website do not include an email form. If you email us, we use your message to respond. A signup endpoint from an earlier page still accepts an address if one is sent: Cloudflare Pages processes that request, Cloudflare KV stores the address and the time it arrived, and Resend may send one message. We use the address only for that message. We retain it until it is no longer needed or you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit an email address. You may also ask us to access or delete your signup information by contacting us below.

Product privacy

Individual Plato Press products may collect different information to provide their features. Each product has its own privacy notice explaining its actual data flow, service providers, retention, and choices. Review that notice before using the product.

External destinations

Links to Citizen Coach, booksellers, and other services take you to sites governed by their own privacy practices.

Contact

Questions or requests may be sent to partnerships@plato-press.com or mailed to 1345 Alma St, Palo Alto, CA 94301.
