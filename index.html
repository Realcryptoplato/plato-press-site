How we work

Small by design.
Useful on purpose.

Plato Press publishes books and develops small, purpose-built products for consequential moments. Our work favors clarity, privacy, and usefulness over engagement for its own sake. Plato Press was founded by Greg Lindberg in Palo Alto, California.
